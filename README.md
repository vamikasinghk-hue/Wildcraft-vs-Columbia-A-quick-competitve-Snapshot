📊 Wildcraft vs Columbia: A Quick Competitive Market Snapshot

Dug into pricing, discounting, and product positioning across Brand Sites, Myntra, and Nykaa Man for outdoor jackets and hiking shirts. A few things stood out:

🔹 Pricing power: Columbia commands a significant premium across every category — Puffer Jackets average ~2x Wildcraft's price, with similar gaps in Fleece Jackets and Hiking Shirts.

🔹 Discounting strategy: Columbia barely discounts (0% on 2 of 3 matched pairs), while Wildcraft leans hard into promotions — up to 53% off in some cases. Classic premium-vs-value positioning playing out in real time.

🔹 Material mix: Polyester dominates the assortment across both brands, followed by Nylon and Polyester Fleece blends.

🔹 Functionality: Puffer Jackets pack the most pockets overall, with a healthy mix of zippered storage — useful signal for feature-based comparisons.

💰 Combined selling price tracked across both brands: ₹71.6K+ in this sample set.

This kind of side-by-side view is a great way to benchmark pricing architecture, promo cadence, and product depth against a competitor — especially in the outdoor/apparel space where perceived value drives conversion.
